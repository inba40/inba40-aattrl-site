Engagements

Most engagements begin with a single problem. Some are a review before a decision, some are hands-on recovery, and some become a standing relationship. The format follows the problem; the people and the terms stay the same.

  • Executive problem-solving

    The core engagement. One problem, a small senior team, worked from brief to resolution.

  • Independent decision review

    A senior, independent view of a major decision before it is taken, addressed to you and shareable with the board.

  • Execution and recovery

    Senior people leading a recovery, a turnaround, a negotiation or a crisis response, alongside your team.

  • Standing counsel

    A continuing, confidential relationship: a sounding board, rapid reviews, and senior people on call.

All four run on the same terms: fixed scope and fee per stage, senior people throughout, confidentiality by default and independence from any product or party. How an engagement runs.

Start with a conversation

Describe the situation in a few lines. The principal replies personally, usually within two working days, and tells you honestly whether AATTRL is the right firm to take it on.
