How the platform works

Five programmes turn the mission into work. All of them are open to members. All of them are starting in the founding phase, and members shape them.

  1. Pathway working groups

    One working group per pathway, with sector sub-groups where needed. Members from every stakeholder group work on specific problems brought by operators: a site, a zone, a process. Groups meet online monthly and in person at convenings. Outputs are published on the platform: case studies, guidance and the problems still open.

  2. Independent transition reviews

    Experienced engineers and researchers from the platform review an organisation’s baseline, target and plan, and say in writing whether they are credible. Reviews are required for Pledge signatories within their first year and available to any member organisation. Reviewers have no commercial stake in the outcome and declare any connection.

  3. Industry–academia projects

    Applied research, pilots and field trials matched between operator members and university members, with providers and innovators where a method is being tested. Results are published whether or not they succeed, because a documented failure saves the next site the cost of repeating it.

  4. Regional chapters

    The platform is global; the work is local. Chapters bring the stakeholders of one region together on its own industry, water, grid and rules. The first chapters are India and the GCC. Further chapters open where members ask for them and will lead them.

  5. Convenings

    A global convening each year, regional roundtables, monthly online pathway sessions and campus sessions at member universities. Formats and the first programme of dates are on the events page.

AATTRL does not charge for membership or programmes in the founding phase. How the platform is funded over the long term, by members, grants and institutional partners, will be decided with the founding members and published.
