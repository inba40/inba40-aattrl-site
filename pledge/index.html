The Pledge

Signing the Pledge is how an organisation takes its place on the platform. It is short, public and specific, and it is followed by an independent review within the first year. It costs nothing to sign and something to keep.

What a signatory commits to

  1. A public baseline

    Within twelve months of signing, publish a baseline of scope 1 and 2 emissions, water withdrawal and waste for the signed scope, with the method stated.

  2. A dated target

    A net-zero target for the signed scope with interim milestones at no more than five-year intervals, and the first actions named.

  3. Nature and water

    Commitments specific to the sites in scope: water returned clean, land and biodiversity around the site measured and protected.

  4. People

    Commitments to the safety, skills and livelihoods of workers and to engagement with the communities beside each site, before decisions rather than after.

  5. Annual progress

    Progress published on the platform every year against the baseline, including where it is behind, and an independent review within the first year.

  6. Shared knowledge

    At least one documented case per year of what was done, what it cost and what it delivered, for other members to use.

What the Pledge means for each stakeholder

Operators
The commitments above, for a site, a business unit or the whole company.
Engineering and technology providers
Published performance data for the solutions supplied, and the baseline and target for the provider's own operations.
Investors and finance
Published share of industrial lending or investment aligned with net-zero and nature goals, and a target for it.
Universities and research
Committed research time to platform working groups and reviews, and the baseline and target for the campus.
Governments and public bodies
Participation in a regional chapter and publication of the industrial-zone or jurisdiction's own baseline and target.

Independent review

Every signatory's baseline, target and plan is reviewed within the first year by platform members with the relevant experience and no stake in the outcome. The review says in writing whether the target is credible and the plan sufficient. A signatory whose plan is found wanting is helped to fix it, not expelled; a signatory that does not publish is removed from the register.

The register

Signatories are listed publicly on the platform with their scope, baseline date, target and latest progress. The register opens with the founding signatories; organisations that sign now are founding signatories.
