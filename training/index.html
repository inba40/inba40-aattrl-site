Technical Training Programmes

Practical programmes delivered by specialists who use these methods on real projects and plants. Every programme can be tailored to your industry, facilities and teams.

Request a WorkshopInvite a Guest Lecturer

How programmes are delivered

Programmes run on site, online or at a venue you choose. Each combines short explanations with worked examples, exercises and discussion of the participants’ own situations. Content is adapted to the client’s industry and, where possible, uses the client’s own procedures and examples.

For in-company programmes, see corporate training. Colleges and universities can invite specialists for guest lectures and industry workshops.

Booking a programme

Send the programme of interest, the number and roles of participants, the preferred dates and the format. A specialist will confirm how the content will be tailored and the arrangements for delivery.

Certificates

Participants receive certificates of attendance issued by AATTRL. Unless stated for a specific programme, courses are not accredited by a professional body.

Frequently asked questions

Can a programme be tailored to our company?

Yes. Programmes are adapted to the client’s industry, facilities and procedures, and can be combined into longer development programmes.

What is the minimum group size?

There is no fixed minimum for in-company programmes. Practical workshops work best with roughly 8 to 20 participants.

Can training be delivered online?

Yes. Most programmes can run online in shorter sessions; exercises such as HAZOP practice work well with screen-shared materials.
