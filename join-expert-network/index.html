Who we are looking for

Senior practitioners with substantial, verifiable experience in engineering, projects, technical safety, operations, maintenance and reliability, asset management, digital and data, industrial AI, cybersecurity, sustainability, management consulting or training. Experience across several countries or industries is valuable, but depth of expertise matters most.

How it works

  1. Submit the application below, with a CV if possible.
  2. We review your experience and may arrange an introductory call.
  3. When a requirement matches your expertise and availability, we contact you with the details before proposing you to a client.
  4. Each assignment is agreed individually. You decide whether to take it.

Your information

Your application is used only to assess your fit with the network and to contact you about relevant assignments. Your name, profile and details are never published without your explicit consent.
