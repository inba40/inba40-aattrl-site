Challenges we take on

These are the problems that stay on a chief executive’s desk because they cross functions, involve people as much as numbers, and carry consequences that no one else is accountable for. Each page describes what the problem usually looks like from the top, and how AATTRL works it through.

  • Growth has stalled

    Revenue has flattened and every explanation is partly true. Sales blames product, product blames pricing, finance blames the market.

  • Margins under pressure

    Margins are thinning, the cost plan is in its third version, and the cuts so far have taken capability without taking cost.

  • A critical programme is failing

    The flagship project is late and over budget, the steering committee reports amber, and the people closest to it have stopped telling you the truth.

  • Operations are not delivering

    Output, service or reliability keep falling short, the operational reasons have been fixed twice, and the problem keeps returning.

  • Transformation is not paying off

    The business has spent heavily on digital, data or AI, the programme reports progress, and you cannot point to where it has paid off.

  • A decision you cannot get wrong

    A major investment, acquisition, market entry, exit or restructuring. The advisers each have an interest, and the board will remember who decided.

  • The leadership team is not executing

    Strategy is agreed in the room and diverges in the corridor. Priorities multiply, decisions are revisited, and you are the only person holding the whole plan.

  • A crisis or turnaround

    A liquidity squeeze, a lost major customer, a serious incident or a business that must be turned around in months, with everyone watching.

  • Board or investor pressure

    The board, an investor or the parent has lost patience. You need an independent view of your own position and a plan they will believe before the next meeting.

Not on the list? Most problems that reach a chief executive are. Describe yours and the principal will tell you honestly whether AATTRL can take it on.
