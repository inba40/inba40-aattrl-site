Asset management for utilities

Utilities were early adopters of formal asset management because their regulators require them to justify investment and performance. Specialists help with maturity assessments against ISO 55001, asset management plans for large populations of assets, condition and risk models, and the asset information needed to support decisions. See asset management consulting.

Reliability and outages

For generators, availability during peak demand and the cost of forced outages drive value. Reliability specialists address repeat failures in boilers, turbines, generators and balance of plant, and outage readiness reviews make sure that major overhauls start with a defined scope, materials on site and contractors ready.

OT security

Power systems are critical national infrastructure and increasingly targeted. OT cybersecurity assessments cover control systems, substation automation and remote access, aligned with IEC 62443 and the regulations that apply in each country.

The energy transition

Utilities are integrating renewables, storage and new loads such as data centres while retiring or repurposing thermal assets. Specialists support grid advisory questions, technical due diligence on acquisitions, and decarbonization planning. For renewable assets, see renewable energy.

Frequently asked questions

Can you help a utility prepare for ISO 55001?

Yes. Support includes maturity assessment, gap closure, development of the policy, SAMP and plans, and preparation for certification audits by an accredited body.

Do you support power plant outages?

Yes. Outage readiness reviews and planning support help define scope, plan resources and materials, and reduce overrun risk.

Do you work on transmission and distribution networks?

Yes, particularly asset management, condition-based investment planning, maintenance strategy and OT security for network operators.
