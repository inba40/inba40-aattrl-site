Facilities and networks

Specialists support drinking water treatment plants, seawater and brackish water desalination, wastewater and sludge treatment, industrial water and effluent treatment, pumping stations and distribution and collection networks.

Asset management

Water utilities manage large, dispersed and ageing asset bases with limited budgets and growing service expectations. Asset management brings structure to these decisions: condition and criticality assessment, risk-based renewal planning, maintenance strategies and the asset information needed to support them.

Energy efficiency

Pumping, aeration and, in desalination, high-pressure pumps and energy recovery account for most energy use. Energy audits identify savings from pump selection and control, aeration control, energy recovery and operating practices, which reduce both cost and emissions.

Reliability and maintenance

Pump, blower and membrane failures directly affect service and compliance. Reliability and maintenance optimization programmes reduce repeat failures and focus maintenance on critical assets.

Chemical safety

Treatment chemicals such as chlorine, sodium hypochlorite, acids, caustic and coagulants need safe storage, dosing and handling. Specialists facilitate HAZOP and HAZID studies of chemical systems and review safety arrangements.

OT security

Water systems are critical infrastructure, and SCADA and remote telemetry are attractive targets. OT cybersecurity assessments identify practical improvements in segmentation, remote access and monitoring.

New plants

For new treatment and desalination plants, specialists provide owner’s engineering, commissioning support and operational readiness, including operator training and maintenance systems before handover.

Typical engagements

  • Asset management maturity assessment for a municipal utility
  • Energy audit of pumping and aeration systems
  • HAZOP of a chlorination system upgrade
  • OT security assessment of a SCADA network
  • Operational readiness for a new desalination plant

Frequently asked questions

Do you support desalination plants?

Yes, including reliability of high-pressure pumps and energy recovery devices, membrane system performance, commissioning and operational readiness.

Can you help a water utility prioritise renewals?

Yes. Risk-based renewal planning combines asset condition, criticality and cost to prioritise investment across networks and treatment assets.

Do you review chemical dosing safety?

Yes. Specialists can facilitate hazard studies and review storage, dosing and handling arrangements for treatment chemicals.
