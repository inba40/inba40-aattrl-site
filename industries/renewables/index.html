Developing, financing or operating solar, wind, battery storage or hydrogen projects? AATTRL provides independent technical specialists for owners, investors and lenders, from design review and due diligence to commissioning and long-term asset performance.

Technologies covered

Solar PV

Utility-scale and commercial plants: energy yield assumptions, module and inverter selection, construction quality and performance testing.

Wind

Onshore wind farms, with support for offshore projects: turbine selection, balance of plant, construction and commissioning, and O&M strategy.

Battery energy storage (BESS)

Technology and supplier selection, safety and fire risk, augmentation and degradation, integration with solar or the grid.

Green hydrogen

Electrolyser selection, balance of plant, process safety and the integration of renewable supply, storage and offtake.

Where independent advice adds value

  • Development and financing. Technical due diligence and independent engineering reviews of design, yield, contracts and budgets for investors and lenders.
  • Construction. Owner’s engineer oversight of EPC and supply contractors, including quality surveillance and progress verification.
  • Commissioning and handover. Witnessing tests and verifying completion, performance and documentation before acceptance. See commissioning and start-up.
  • Operations. O&M strategy, performance reviews, reliability analysis of inverters, turbines and balance of plant, and asset management for portfolios.

Safety in renewable and storage projects

Renewable projects carry their own hazards: battery thermal runaway and fire, electrical hazards, work at height and lifting, and the flammability of hydrogen. Specialists facilitate HAZID studies and review safety designs against recognised references, such as NFPA 855 for stationary energy storage systems and test data such as UL 9540A, along with local fire authority requirements.

Hybrid and grid-integrated projects

Increasingly, projects combine solar, wind and storage, and sometimes hydrogen, behind a single grid connection. Specialists review the integrated design, control strategy and grid compliance, and the commercial assumptions that depend on them.

Typical engagements

  • Buy-side technical due diligence on an operating solar portfolio
  • Owner’s engineer for a BESS project through construction and commissioning
  • HAZID for a green hydrogen demonstration plant
  • Performance review of an underperforming wind farm
  • Asset management framework for a growing renewables portfolio

Frequently asked questions

Do you act as independent engineer for lenders on renewable projects?

AATTRL can provide independent technical advisors for lenders, subject to confirming independence and the qualifications and insurance the lender requires.

Can you review BESS fire safety?

Yes. Specialists review battery storage safety design, hazard studies and emergency response arrangements against recognised references and local requirements.

Do you support operating renewable assets?

Yes. Support includes performance reviews, reliability analysis, O&M strategy and contract reviews, and asset management for portfolios.
