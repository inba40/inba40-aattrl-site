Commissioning

Data centre commissioning typically follows a levelled approach: factory acceptance testing, site inspection, component start-up, functional performance testing and integrated systems testing (IST), in which the facility is tested as a whole under simulated load and failure scenarios. Specialists plan commissioning, review scripts, witness tests and track issues to closure. Frameworks such as the Uptime Institute Tier classification and ANSI/TIA-942 are often used to define the resilience the design and testing must demonstrate.

From construction to operation

Many data centre incidents happen early in operation, when procedures, training and maintenance programmes are new. Operational readiness planning makes sure that method statements and emergency operating procedures are written and practised, maintenance programmes are in the CMMS, spares are on site and staff have been trained on the actual installed equipment before go-live.

Energy and sustainability

Power and cooling dominate operating cost and emissions. Specialists assess cooling strategy, controls and operating set points, and track efficiency using metrics such as PUE, defined in ISO/IEC 30134-2.

Typical engagements

  • Operational readiness assessment before go-live
  • Resilience review to identify single points of failure
  • Fire protection review for lithium-ion UPS batteries
  • Cooling efficiency assessment for an operating facility

Frequently asked questions

What is integrated systems testing (IST)?

The final level of data centre commissioning, in which power, cooling, controls and monitoring are tested together under simulated load and failure conditions to prove the facility performs as designed.

Can you support operational readiness for a new data centre?

Yes. Readiness covers organisation, procedures, maintenance programmes, CMMS, spares, training and handover from the construction and commissioning teams.

Do you advise on PUE improvement?

Yes. Energy specialists review cooling design and control, set points, airflow management and opportunities for free cooling and heat reuse.
