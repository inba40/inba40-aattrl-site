The challenges of petrochemical operations

Petrochemical facilities, including steam crackers, aromatics units, polymer plants and specialty chemical sites, share several characteristics: reactive and sometimes toxic chemistry, high-temperature furnaces, large rotating equipment, complex utility systems and tight product specifications. They also face pressure to reduce energy use and emissions while maintaining safe, reliable operation.

Process safety

Reactive hazards, such as runaway reactions, unwanted polymerisation and decomposition, require experience beyond a standard HAZOP. Specialists support HAZOP studies of batch and continuous processes, LOPA and SIL assessments for reactor protection, relief and flare system reviews, and process safety management assessments. Studies take account of the client’s own risk criteria and the major accident regulations that apply to the site.

Reliability and turnarounds

Cracking furnaces, cracked gas compressors, extruders and pumps in demanding services are frequent sources of downtime. Reliability programmes identify bad actors and root causes; maintenance optimization ensures that preventive and predictive work is focused where it matters. Turnaround readiness reviews check scope, planning, materials and contractor readiness well before the shutdown.

Integrity

High temperatures, corrosive streams and cyclic operation create damage mechanisms such as high-temperature corrosion, creep, fatigue and stress corrosion cracking. Asset integrity specialists develop risk-based inspection programmes, integrity operating windows and fitness-for-service assessments.

Energy and emissions

Furnaces, steam systems and compressors make petrochemicals energy-intensive. Energy efficiency and decarbonization work identifies heat recovery, steam system improvements, electrification options and operational savings, and builds them into a credible roadmap. Process models and digital twins help test operating changes before they are made.

Typical engagements

  • Reactive hazard review and HAZOP for a new specialty chemical unit
  • LOPA and SIL revalidation after a capacity increase
  • Bad actor programme for rotating equipment across a site
  • Turnaround readiness review six months before a major shutdown
  • Energy audit of a steam and furnace system

Frequently asked questions

Do you cover batch chemical processes?

Yes. HAZOP and LOPA specialists can review batch processes step by step, including charging, reaction, transfer and cleaning operations.

Can you help prepare for a turnaround?

Yes. Turnaround readiness reviews assess scope definition and freeze, planning quality, materials, contractors and safety preparation, with recommendations before execution.

Do you support petrochemical projects as well as operating plants?

Yes, including design reviews, owner’s engineering, commissioning and operational readiness for new units and expansions.
