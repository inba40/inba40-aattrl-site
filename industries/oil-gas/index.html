Where specialists support oil and gas operators

Segment Typical facilities Frequent requests
Upstream Onshore and offshore production facilities, gas processing, water injection HAZID and HAZOP, brownfield modifications, integrity of ageing facilities, operational readiness for new developments
Midstream Pipelines, compression, storage, terminals Pipeline and station integrity, compressor reliability, OT cybersecurity, commissioning of new stations
Downstream Refineries, gas plants, fuel terminals Process safety studies, turnaround readiness, reliability improvement, energy efficiency

Common challenges

  • Ageing assets. Many facilities operate well beyond their original design life, with changed duties and incomplete records. Asset integrity and life-extension work establish what is safe and what must change.
  • Brownfield modifications. Tie-ins, debottlenecking and new equipment in live plants need careful hazard studies and interface management. See HAZOP and HAZID.
  • Project delivery. Owners delivering through EPC contractors need independent design reviews and an owner’s engineer on their side.
  • Start-up. New facilities need commissioning, operational readiness and a rigorous pre-start-up safety review.
  • Reliability and cost. Repeat failures of pumps, compressors and fired equipment erode margins; reliability work removes their causes.
  • Data and digital. Engineering, maintenance and process data spread across systems slows every decision; see digital asset transformation.
  • Emissions. Flaring, venting, methane and energy use are under scrutiny from regulators, investors and buyers; see decarbonization.

Typical engagements

  • Independent HAZOP facilitation for a gas processing expansion
  • FEED review before a final investment decision
  • Owner’s engineer support during EPC execution
  • Operational readiness assessment for a new onshore facility
  • Bad actor and RCA programme for rotating equipment
  • Risk-based inspection programme for an ageing processing plant
  • Digital roadmap linking engineering data, EAM and historian

Working with operators’ standards

Specialists work to the client’s own standards and to recognised industry references, such as API recommended practices, ISO standards for the petroleum and natural gas industries, IOGP guidance, and the requirements of national oil companies and regulators. Where an operator has its own HAZOP, MOC or integrity procedures, the work follows them.

Delivery

AATTRL supports oil and gas clients in India, the GCC and the wider Middle East, Europe, Asia and Africa, with remote and onsite delivery depending on the assignment.

Frequently asked questions

Can AATTRL support national oil company projects?

Yes. Specialists have worked to national oil company standards and procedures, and work within the client’s approval and vendor registration requirements.

Do you support offshore as well as onshore facilities?

Yes, for studies, reviews and advisory work. Offshore site work depends on the specialist’s certification for offshore travel, which is confirmed for each assignment.

Can you provide a specialist at short notice?

Often, yes. Tell us the requirement, timing and location, and we will confirm availability before proposing a specialist.
