Home/Stakeholders/Governments and regulators

For the people who set the rules

Regulation works best when the regulated understand it and the regulator understands what is possible. The platform offers a working relationship between the two that is neither lobbying nor enforcement: shared evidence, early dialogue and policy tested against real sites.

What you bring

Authority, timelines, incentives and the public interest.

What you get

  • Evidence from pledged sites about what measures cost and deliver, to inform standards and incentives.
  • Early dialogue with operators, providers and financiers on proposed rules, before positions harden.
  • Independent technical input from engineers and researchers with no commercial stake.
  • Regional chapters, starting in India and the GCC, where industrial-zone authorities, ministries and industry can work on the same plan.

How to take part

Government and regulatory bodies take part as observers or members of regional chapters and working groups. Contact the platform to discuss participation.

What this stakeholder needs from the others

  • Honest data from operators
  • Independent technical advice
  • A forum that is not a lobby
