For the people who run industry

Operators carry the transition. They also carry the production targets, the maintenance backlog and the balance sheet. The platform exists to make the transition the operator’s easiest path, not an extra burden on top of everything else.

What you bring

The assets, the data, the operating knowledge and the decisions. Nothing moves until an operator decides it will.

What you get

  • A structured way to set a baseline, a dated target and a plan, using the Pledge framework, without hiring a consultancy to tell you what it is.
  • Independent review of that plan by engineers and researchers who have no product to sell you.
  • Direct access to technology providers, financiers and universities working on the problems your sites have, in working groups by pathway and sector.
  • Case material from other operators: what was done, what it cost, what it saved, what went wrong.
  • A voice in the regulatory conversation, alongside the regulators, rather than after them.

How to take part

Sign the Pledge on behalf of a site, a business unit or the whole company. Nominate the people who will represent you in working groups. Share one thing that has worked.

What this stakeholder needs from the others

  • Engineering and technology providers with proven methods
  • Finance structured for long-payback efficiency and electrification
  • Regulators who set clear, stable rules
  • Universities to validate methods and train people

Other stakeholders

  • Engineering, technology and solution providers
  • Investors and finance
  • Governments and regulators
  • Universities and research
  • Professionals and experts
  • Workers and communities
  • Startups and innovators
