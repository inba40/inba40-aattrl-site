Eight stakeholders, one platform

Each group holds a piece of the transition. None can finish it alone. Each page describes what that stakeholder brings, what it needs from the others, and how to take part.

  • Industry operators and asset owners

    The companies that own and run plants, networks, fleets and mines. The transition happens on their sites or not at all.

  • Engineering, technology and solution providers

    EPCs, OEMs, engineering consultancies, digital and process technology companies. They turn intent into installed change.

  • Investors and finance

    Banks, funds, development finance, insurers and corporate treasuries. The transition is capital-intensive and they decide what gets funded.

  • Governments and regulators

    Ministries, regulators, industrial zones and municipalities. They set the rules, the incentives and the timelines.

  • Universities and research

    Research groups, institutes and teaching departments. They prove what works, train who does it, and keep everyone honest.

  • Professionals and experts

    Engineers, scientists, safety and environmental specialists, project and operations leaders. The transition is delivered by people with experience.

  • Workers and communities

    The people who work in industry and the people who live beside it. They carry the impact and should share the benefit.

  • Startups and innovators

    New companies with new methods: materials, processes, sensors, software, biology. They need sites to prove it on.
