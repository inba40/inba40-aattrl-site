For the people with the new idea

Most industrial innovation fails not in the laboratory but in the gap between a demonstration and a plant that will try it. The platform is built to close that gap: operators with a stated problem, universities to validate, investors watching, and results published.

What you bring

New methods and the willingness to test them where it is hard.

What you get

  • Operators’ stated problems, by pathway and sector, before anyone has written a tender.
  • Pilot and field-trial opportunities with pledged operators and university partners.
  • Independent validation of results, published on the platform.
  • Visibility to investors who are looking for exactly this.

The rule

The same as for established providers: contributors, not sponsors. Standing comes from reviewed results.

How to take part

Join and describe what you have and what you need to prove it.

What this stakeholder needs from the others

  • A site willing to pilot
  • Independent validation
  • Patient capital
