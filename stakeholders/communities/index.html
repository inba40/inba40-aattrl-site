Home/Stakeholders/Workers and communities

For the people who live with it

Industry’s impact lands first on the people who work inside it and the people who live around it. A transition designed without them tends to fail, or to succeed at their expense. The platform gives workers and communities a seat at the table, not a consultation at the end.

What you bring

Lived knowledge of the site and its effects, the skills that run it, and the legitimacy without which no transition lasts.

What you get

  • A place in working groups on the pathways that affect you most: people and just transition, water and ecosystems, nature-positive sites.
  • Plain-language information about pledged sites in your area: what they have committed to and how they are doing.
  • A channel to operators and regulators that does not depend on protest.
  • Skills and training routes as jobs change, through the platform’s university and operator members.

How to take part

Join individually, or as an organisation representing workers or a community. Membership is free.

What this stakeholder needs from the others

  • Information in plain language
  • Operators who engage before deciding
  • Training for the jobs that come next

Other stakeholders

  • Industry operators and asset owners
  • Engineering, technology and solution providers
  • Investors and finance
  • Governments and regulators
  • Universities and research
  • Professionals and experts
  • Startups and innovators
