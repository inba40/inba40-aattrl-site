Home/Stakeholders/Universities and research

For the people who prove it

Universities hold the methods, the measurement and the next generation of engineers. What they often lack is access: to sites, to data, to the problems industry actually has. The platform is built to close that gap in both directions.

What you bring

Research capability, measurement and validation, independent judgement, and students.

What you get

  • Real industrial problems, posed by operators in working groups, with sites and data to work on.
  • A role in independent review of transition plans and technology performance.
  • Industry partners for applied projects, pilots and field trials.
  • A route for research findings to reach the people who can install them.
  • Guest lectures, industry projects and placements for students, drawn from the platform’s members.

How to take part

Join individually as a researcher or lecturer, or sign the Pledge as an institution, which for universities means committing research time to platform working groups and reviews.

What this stakeholder needs from the others

  • Access to sites and operating data
  • Industry partners for applied research
  • Funding routes for pilots

Other stakeholders

  • Industry operators and asset owners
  • Engineering, technology and solution providers
  • Investors and finance
  • Governments and regulators
  • Professionals and experts
  • Workers and communities
  • Startups and innovators

Take your place on the platform

Join as an individual, or sign the Pledge on behalf of your organisation. Both are free in the founding phase.
