Industry can run within nature's limits. It takes all of us.

Industry made the modern world and is responsible for about a quarter of the emissions now changing it. Fixing that is not one company's job, one government's job or one technology's job. AATTRL is a platform where every stakeholder of industry works on it together.

Three horizons, one direction

  • Net zero

    Industrial emissions brought to net zero: energy efficiency first, then electrification and clean fuels, with removals only for what cannot be avoided.

  • Close to nature

    Sites that give back more than they take: water returned clean, land restored, biodiversity protected and measured like any other asset.

  • Zero impact

    Materials kept in use, waste designed out, and no harm to the people who work in industry or live beside it.

Built for every stakeholder, not just the operator

A plant does not reach net zero alone. It needs the engineer who designs the change, the investor who funds it, the regulator who sets the rule, the university that proves the method, the worker who runs it and the community that lives with it. AATTRL gives each of them a place on the same platform.

  • Industry operators and asset owners

    The companies that own and run plants, networks, fleets and mines. The transition happens on their sites or not at all.

  • Engineering, technology and solution providers

    EPCs, OEMs, engineering consultancies, digital and process technology companies. They turn intent into installed change.

  • Investors and finance

    Banks, funds, development finance, insurers and corporate treasuries. The transition is capital-intensive and they decide what gets funded.

  • Governments and regulators

    Ministries, regulators, industrial zones and municipalities. They set the rules, the incentives and the timelines.

  • Universities and research

    Research groups, institutes and teaching departments. They prove what works, train who does it, and keep everyone honest.

  • Professionals and experts

    Engineers, scientists, safety and environmental specialists, project and operations leaders. The transition is delivered by people with experience.

  • Workers and communities

    The people who work in industry and the people who live beside it. They carry the impact and should share the benefit.

  • Startups and innovators

    New companies with new methods: materials, processes, sensors, software, biology. They need sites to prove it on.

What the platform does

Convenes
Brings stakeholders who rarely sit together into working groups, roundtables and convenings, by pathway, by sector and by region.
Commits
Asks organisations to sign the AATTRL Pledge: a public baseline, a dated target, annual progress and a commitment to nature and people.
Shares
Collects what has worked on real sites, in plain language, and makes it available to anyone who will use it.
Verifies
Connects experienced engineers and researchers to review transition plans independently, so targets are real and progress is honest.

The five programmes

Six pathways

One direction, several roads. Each has a working group, a body of knowledge and its own measures of progress.

  • Net-zero operations

    Energy efficiency, process heat, electrification and the honest handling of what cannot yet be avoided.

  • Clean energy and fuels

    Renewable power, storage, green hydrogen and the fuels that will replace gas and coal in industrial processes.

  • Circular materials and zero waste

    Design for reuse, remanufacture and recycling; waste designed out of processes; by-products that become inputs.

  • Water and ecosystems

    Water drawn responsibly and returned clean; rivers, aquifers, coasts and wetlands protected around industrial sites.

  • Nature-positive sites

    Industrial sites designed into their landscape: habitats, corridors, soil and restoration treated as part of the asset.

  • People and just transition

    Workers and communities through the change: safety, health, skills, livelihoods and a fair share of the benefit.

The founding phase

AATTRL is being built in the open. The first members, working groups and pledge signatories are being gathered now, starting in India and the GCC, where industry is growing fastest and the stakes are highest. If you work in, with, for or beside industry, this is the moment to join.

The Pledge

Organisations join by signing the AATTRL Pledge: a public baseline within a year, a dated target with interim milestones, annual progress, commitments to nature and people, and one shared case a year. Reviewed independently. Free to sign; something to keep.
