    Engagement Models

    Choose the engagement that fits the work. Many clients start small and extend as their needs become clear.

    • Expert Hour

      By the hour

      A focused specialist discussion or short technical consultation.

      Second opinion on a design issue, scoping a study, preparing for a client or regulator meeting.

    • Expert Day

      By the day

      Workshops, reviews or onsite support.

      Design review day, site visit, facilitated workshop, technical interview panel.

    • Fixed-Scope Study

      Fixed study

      Defined scope and deliverables.

      HAZOP, audit, readiness assessment, engineering review, due diligence report.

    • Project Assignment

      Weeks or months

      Short- or medium-term project support.

      Commissioning lead, operational readiness lead, interim project engineer.

    • Fractional Specialist

      Part-time

      Part-time senior expertise.

      Reliability lead two days a week, part-time asset management advisor.

    • Advisory Retainer

      Ongoing

      Continuous access to specialist support.

      Monthly advisory hours, technical review on call, board or investment committee support.

    How an Engagement Works

    From first enquiry to delivered assignment.

    1. Tell us the problem

      Share the scope, project stage, location and timing. A short description is enough to start.

    2. Clarify the requirement

      A specialist-led call confirms objectives, constraints and confidentiality needs. An NDA can be signed first.

    3. Propose the right expertise

      You receive the proposed specialist or team, approach, deliverables and fee basis.

    4. Deliver

      Remote, onsite or hybrid delivery, with progress visible and decisions documented.

    5. Close out and transfer knowledge

      Deliverables, action registers and a handover that leaves your team able to carry on.
