The problems that stay on your desk.

Every chief executive carries a few problems that no department can solve and no report quite explains. AATTRL takes one of them on, puts senior people around it, and works it through to a resolution you can act on.

Bring a challengeHow an engagement runs
  • Growth has flattened and every explanation I get is partly true.
  • The programme is a year late and nobody in the room will say so.
  • Margins are thinning and the cost plan is already in its third version.
  • We have spent heavily on digital and I cannot see where it is paying off.
  • This is the one decision this year I cannot afford to get wrong.
  • My leadership team agrees in the meeting and diverges in the corridor.

If one of these sounds like yours, it is the kind of problem AATTRL takes on. See the challenges we take on.

One problem. Senior people. A resolution.

  1. You bring the problem

    A confidential conversation with the principal. You describe the situation as you see it, including the parts that are political, personal or unproven.

  2. We put the right senior people around it

    A small team drawn from the AATTRL senior network: people who have run the function, the project or the business you are describing. No junior staff, no bench to fill.

  3. We work it through to resolution

    Diagnosis you can trust, a decision with its consequences made plain, and the execution support to make it hold. You stay in control; the problem leaves your desk.

Challenges we take on

Named by the problem as it reaches the chief executive, not by the discipline that might address it.

  • Growth has stalled

    Revenue has flattened and every explanation is partly true. Sales blames product, product blames pricing, finance blames the market.

  • Margins under pressure

    Margins are thinning, the cost plan is in its third version, and the cuts so far have taken capability without taking cost.

  • A critical programme is failing

    The flagship project is late and over budget, the steering committee reports amber, and the people closest to it have stopped telling you the truth.

  • Operations are not delivering

    Output, service or reliability keep falling short, the operational reasons have been fixed twice, and the problem keeps returning.

  • Transformation is not paying off

    The business has spent heavily on digital, data or AI, the programme reports progress, and you cannot point to where it has paid off.

  • A decision you cannot get wrong

    A major investment, acquisition, market entry, exit or restructuring. The advisers each have an interest, and the board will remember who decided.

How this differs from hiring a consultancy

Scoped to a problem, not a practice
The engagement ends when the problem is resolved or the decision is made, not when a phase plan says so.
Senior throughout
The people who diagnose are the people who deliver. Nothing is handed down to an analyst team you never meet.
Answerable to you
AATTRL works for the chief executive, not for a function or a sponsor. Findings are reported to you first and in full.
Built for the awkward problems
The ones that involve people, politics and incomplete information as much as numbers. Those are the ones that stay on a CEO's desk.
