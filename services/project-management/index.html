Project support that fits the gap

Most organisations do not need a large consultancy team to fix a project. They need one or two experienced people in the right roles: a project manager to restore control, a scheduler who can build a realistic integrated schedule, or a cost engineer who can explain where the money is going. AATTRL provides those specialists for as long as they are needed.

Services

Project management and PMC support

Interim project managers and project directors, owner’s project management, and project management consultancy support to owners’ teams.

Project controls

Work breakdown and cost breakdown structures, progress measurement, earned value, change control and reporting that management can act on.

Planning and scheduling

Integrated schedules covering engineering, procurement, construction and commissioning; schedule quality checks and critical path analysis.

Cost engineering

Estimate reviews, cost control, forecasting, contingency analysis and cost risk assessment.

Interface management

Interface registers and agreements between contractors, packages and existing facilities, with regular interface reviews.

Procurement support

Procurement strategy, technical bid evaluation, expediting and vendor surveillance for critical equipment.

Stage-gate and health checks

An independent project health check looks at scope definition, schedule realism, cost forecast, risk management, interfaces, contracting and organisation, and gives leadership a clear view of whether the project is ready to pass its next gate. It is often requested before a final investment decision, when a project is drifting, or when a new project director takes over.

Setting up a PMO

For owners running several projects, specialists can help set up a project management office: governance and stage gates, standard reporting, planning and cost procedures, and the tools that support them.

Information required

The project charter or scope, current schedule and cost reports, contract strategy and contracts, the risk register and the organisation chart, plus the questions management wants answered.

Deliverables

  • Project execution plans and procedures
  • Integrated schedules and schedule quality reports
  • Cost reports, forecasts and estimate reviews
  • Risk and interface registers
  • Health-check reports with prioritised recommendations

Project specialists work closely with the owner’s engineer and with design assurance reviewers. For the full lifecycle view, see From FEED to Operations.

Frequently asked questions

Can you provide an interim project manager?

Yes. Interim project managers and project controls leads can join for a phase of the project or until a permanent appointment is made.

What does a project health check cover?

Scope, schedule, cost, risk, interfaces, contracts, organisation and reporting. The output is a short report with clear findings and recommendations for leadership.

Which scheduling tools do your planners use?

Planners work in the tools the project already uses, typically Primavera P6 or Microsoft Project for engineering and construction schedules.
