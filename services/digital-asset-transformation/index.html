The problem digital transformation should solve

Most industrial operators already own good software. The difficulty is that each system was bought for one department and holds its own version of the asset. Engineering drawings say one thing, the maintenance system another, the historian uses different tag names, and inspection data sits in a separate database or a spreadsheet. Engineers spend hours finding and reconciling information before they can make a decision. Digital asset transformation fixes the connections, the data and the ways of working, so that the right information is available when it is needed.

Services

Digital maturity assessment

A review of systems, data quality, integration and ways of working, measured against what operations, maintenance and engineering actually need.

Roadmap and business case

A sequenced plan of initiatives with costs, benefits and dependencies, starting with data foundations.

Engineering data management

Asset information requirements, handover specifications for new projects using approaches such as CFIHOS, and clean-up of legacy data.

EAM and APM improvement

Asset hierarchy and master data quality, maintenance plan structure, and asset performance management (APM) requirements and selection.

Data governance

Ownership, standards, quality rules and the routines that keep data correct after the project ends.

Implementation assurance

Independent review of system integrator plans, requirements and testing, so the delivered solution matches the business need.

Vendor-neutral by design

AATTRL does not resell software. Specialists have worked with major platforms, including SAP, IBM Maximo, AVEVA, Hexagon and the PI System, and recommend what fits the client’s existing landscape, skills and budget. Often the best answer is to use current systems better before buying new ones.

Standards that help

Recognised standards make data easier to exchange and maintain: ISO 14224 for reliability and maintenance data in the petroleum, petrochemical and natural gas industries, the CFIHOS specification for capital project information handover, and ISO 19650 for information management on built-environment and infrastructure projects.

Information required

A list of current systems and interfaces, sample data extracts, the asset register structure, known pain points from users, and any existing strategy or roadmap.

Deliverables

  • Current-state system and data map
  • Target architecture and data model principles
  • Prioritised roadmap with business case
  • Data standards and governance model
  • Requirements for new systems or integrations

Digital transformation should serve the asset management system rather than replace it, and connected operational technology must be secured; see OT cybersecurity.

Frequently asked questions

Where should a digital asset transformation start?

With the decisions that matter most and the data they depend on. Fixing the asset register, master data and tag mapping usually delivers more value than adding a new platform.

Are you tied to a software vendor?

No. AATTRL is vendor-neutral and does not resell software. Recommendations are based on the client’s needs and existing systems.

What is asset performance management (APM)?

APM combines condition, reliability, maintenance and risk data to help decide what work to do on which assets and when. It can be a dedicated software product or a set of capabilities built on existing systems.

Can you help a new project plan its digital handover?

Yes. Defining asset information requirements and a handover specification early in the project is far cheaper than cleaning data after start-up.
