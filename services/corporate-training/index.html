Engineering Corporate Training and Technical Workshops

Looking for engineering training delivered by people who do the work, not just teach it? AATTRL organises corporate training and technical workshops led by practising specialists, tailored to your industry, your plants and your teams.

Training that changes how people work

Effective technical training uses real examples, lets participants practise, and connects directly to the work they will do next week. AATTRL training is delivered by specialists who apply these methods on projects and plants, and is adapted to the client’s industry, procedures and current challenges.

Programme areas

Process safety

HAZOP and HAZID for participants and facilitators, LOPA, management of change, PSSR and process safety leadership.

Reliability and maintenance

Root cause analysis, reliability engineering fundamentals, RCM, maintenance planning and scheduling, and turnaround readiness.

Asset management

Asset management principles and ISO 55001, lifecycle cost, risk-based decision-making and asset management plans.

Projects

Project lifecycle and stage gates, owner’s engineering, interface management, commissioning and handover.

Digital engineering and industrial AI

Digital asset management, engineering data, digital twins and practical industrial AI for engineers and managers.

Graduate and early-career programmes

Industrial fundamentals, plant familiarisation, engineering practice and professional skills for new engineers.

The full list of standard programmes, with typical durations, is on the training page.

Formats

  • Technical workshops of half a day to two days on a focused topic
  • Courses of three to five days with exercises and assessments
  • Executive masterclasses for leaders who need to understand risks and decisions rather than methods
  • Technical mentoring for engineers taking on new responsibilities
  • Blended programmes combining online sessions with on-site practical work

How a programme is designed

  1. Understand needs. Agree objectives, participants, current skill levels and constraints.
  2. Tailor content. Adapt materials and examples to the client’s industry, facilities and procedures.
  3. Deliver. Run sessions with practical exercises, case studies and discussion.
  4. Assess. Check learning through exercises or short assessments where appropriate.
  5. Follow up. Provide feedback, recommendations for further development and optional mentoring.

Certificates and accreditation

Participants receive certificates of attendance issued by AATTRL. Unless stated otherwise for a specific programme, courses are not accredited by a professional body. Where an organisation needs accredited training or continuing professional development records, requirements can be discussed during planning.

Information required

Training objectives, number and roles of participants, preferred dates, format and location, and any relevant company procedures or case material.

Deliverables

  • Training needs summary and tailored programme outline
  • Course materials and exercises
  • Delivery by practising specialists
  • Attendance records, assessment results where used, and certificates of attendance
  • Post-training feedback and recommendations

Frequently asked questions

Can training be tailored to our plants and procedures?

Yes. Most programmes are adapted to the client’s industry, facilities and procedures, using the client’s own examples where they can be shared.

Is training delivered online or on site?

Both. Workshops and courses can be delivered on site, online or at a venue you choose, depending on the content and group size.

Are your courses accredited?

Participants receive AATTRL certificates of attendance. Courses are not accredited by a professional body unless this is stated for a specific programme.

What group size works best?

For practical workshops, around 8 to 20 participants allows discussion and exercises. Larger groups suit awareness sessions and masterclasses.
