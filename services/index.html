Safety, Risk & Assurance

Independent facilitators and experienced technical safety engineers help identify, assess and manage major accident hazards. Studies are run to recognised methods, recorded to an auditable standard and followed through to action close-out.

Request a specialist

Dedicated service pages

  • HAZOPIndependent HAZOP chairs and scribes for new designs, modifications and revalidations.
  • HAZIDStructured hazard identification workshops from concept selection to brownfield change.
  • LOPA & SILLayer of protection analysis, SIL targets and functional safety support after HAZOP.
  • PSSRIndependent pre-start-up safety reviews before hazardous materials or energy are introduced.
  • Technical SafetyHazard studies, risk assessment, safety cases, fire protection and process safety management.
