A network, not a single consultant

AATTRL is a platform that represents a multidisciplinary network of specialists. When a requirement arrives, it is matched with the specialist, or the combination of specialists, whose experience fits the problem, the industry and the stage of the project. No single person is presented as covering every discipline.

Who the specialists are

Network specialists are experienced practitioners: engineers, operators, project leaders, safety professionals, digital specialists and advisors who have delivered work for asset owners, operators, contractors and investors. Many have worked internationally, across India, the GCC, Europe and other regions.

How matching works

  1. Tell us the problem

    Share the scope, project stage, location and timing. A short description is enough to start.

  2. Clarify the requirement

    A specialist-led call confirms objectives, constraints and confidentiality needs. An NDA can be signed first.

  3. Propose the right expertise

    You receive the proposed specialist or team, approach, deliverables and fee basis.

  4. Deliver

    Remote, onsite or hybrid delivery, with progress visible and decisions documented.

  5. Close out and transfer knowledge

    Deliverables, action registers and a handover that leaves your team able to carry on.

How specialists are proposed

Before a specialist is proposed, their relevant experience is checked against the requirement, their availability is confirmed and any conflict of interest with the parties involved is identified. Where an assignment needs particular qualifications, registrations or site certifications, these are confirmed for that assignment.

Services are delivered by appropriately qualified and experienced specialists according to project, jurisdiction and client requirements.

Confidentiality and independence

Client information is shared only with the specialists who need it to respond, and non-disclosure agreements can be put in place before details are exchanged. Specialists give independent advice and do not receive commissions from equipment or software vendors for their recommendations.

Are you a senior specialist?

Experienced engineers, operators and advisors can apply to join the network. Personal details are never published without consent.

Frequently asked questions

How quickly can a specialist start?

It depends on the discipline, location and duration. Short remote consultations can often be arranged within days; onsite and longer assignments need more notice. Availability is confirmed before any proposal.

Can several specialists work together?

Yes. Multidisciplinary teams and expert panels are formed when a requirement crosses disciplines, for example a design review or an investment decision.

Who contracts with the client?

The contracting arrangement is agreed for each engagement and set out in the proposal, including scope, fee basis, confidentiality and responsibilities.

Can we speak with the specialist before confirming?

Yes. A short introductory call is normally arranged so you can confirm that the specialist’s experience fits your requirement.
