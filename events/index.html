Convenings

The platform meets in four formats. The first programme of dates will be published here and sent to members; join to be told.

Global convening
Once a year, in person with online participation. All stakeholders, all pathways: the state of the transition, the year's reviewed cases, the open problems, and the next year's working-group agenda. The first will be held in India or the GCC.
Regional roundtables
Chapter-level, several times a year. The stakeholders of one region on its own industry, water, grid and rules. Small enough to work; attended by the people who decide.
Pathway sessions
Monthly, online, one per pathway. A site or a problem brought by an operator, worked through by the group. Open to all members of the working group.
Campus sessions
At member universities: lectures and workshops by industry members for students and faculty, and research presentations to industry. Arranged with each university's department.

Members can propose a roundtable or a campus session for their region or institution through the platform.
