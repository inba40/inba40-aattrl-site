What AATTRL is

AATTRL Global Expert Advisory is a platform for specialist expertise on demand. It represents a multidisciplinary network of experienced specialists rather than a single consultant, and it exists for a simple reason: many of the most important technical needs in industry are specialised, urgent and temporary. Building a permanent team for each of them is slow and expensive. Accessing the right specialist for the right period is not.

How the platform works

Clients describe the problem. AATTRL matches it with a specialist or a multidisciplinary team whose experience fits the industry, the discipline and the stage of the work, confirms availability and independence, and agrees a clear scope and engagement model. Work can be delivered remotely, on site or in a hybrid format, by the hour, day, study, project or retainer.

See the expert network for engagement models and specialist categories.

Where we work

AATTRL supports clients in India, the GCC and the wider Middle East, Europe, Asia and Africa, and worldwide where an assignment can be delivered remotely or the right specialist can travel. Each assignment is planned around the client’s location, regulatory context and access requirements.

Independence and confidentiality

Specialists give independent advice focused on the client’s requirements. AATTRL does not resell equipment or software. Information shared with AATTRL is treated as confidential, shared only with the specialists who need it, and can be covered by a non-disclosure agreement before details are exchanged. See the confidentiality statement.

Qualified specialists for regulated work

Services are delivered by appropriately qualified and experienced specialists according to project, jurisdiction and client requirements.

Where a service requires a licence, registration, accreditation or statutory authority, for example design sign-off, statutory inspection, certification audits or emissions verification, this is confirmed for the specific assignment or the work is carried out by an appropriately authorised party.

Frequently asked questions

Is AATTRL a single consultant?

No. AATTRL is a platform representing a multidisciplinary network of specialists. Each requirement is matched with the specialist or team whose experience fits it.

Where is AATTRL based?

AATTRL works as a distributed network and can be contacted by email at info@aattrl.com or on WhatsApp at +971 50 607 4374. Specialists work remotely and on site depending on the assignment.

How are engagements priced?

Each engagement has an agreed scope and fee basis: hourly, daily, fixed price for a defined study, or a monthly retainer, set out in the proposal before work starts.
